🫀 CPR for Unresponsive Adults ↓
1. Call 911 or activate ResQ SOS.
2. Place hands center of chest, push hard and fast (100–120 beats/min to the beat of "Stayin' Alive").
3. Perform 30 chest compressions followed by 2 rescue breaths if trained.
🩸 Severe Bleeding & Tourniquet ↓
1. Apply direct firm pressure with clean cloth or dressing.
2. If limb hemorrhage does not stop, apply tourniquet 5–7cm above wound (never over a joint).
3. Tighten until bleeding stops. Record the exact application time on the patient's forehead.
💧 Emergency Water Purification ↓
• Boiling: Bring cloudy water to a vigorous rolling boil for at least 1 full minute.
• Disinfection: Add 2 drops of standard unscented household bleach per liter of clear water. Stir and wait 30 minutes before drinking.
