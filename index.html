Verification & Trust

Combating Disaster Rumors & False Alarms

During chaos, misinformation can cost lives. AegisLink applies a rigorous 4-tier verification taxonomy and cryptographically signed public alerts.

OFFICIAL ALERT Signed ED25519

Authorized Authority Broadcast

Directly issued by certified civil defense, meteorological departments, or government disaster agencies. Cannot be spoofed.

Example: "Mandatory evacuation ordered for low-lying Sector 4 basin."
VERIFIED INCIDENT Responder Confirmed

First-Responder / Drone Verified

Field responders, certified observers, or reconnaissance drones have physically confirmed the hazard on-scene.

Example: "Sector 4 bridge structural damage confirmed. Detour routes active."
SENSOR DATA Hardware Telemetry

Automated IoT Sensor Telemetry

Derived directly from hardware sensors (ultrasonic water gauges, seismic IMUs) with calibrated threshold alerts.

Example: "Node #014 water level ultrasonic sensor exceeded 4.5m mark."
COMMUNITY REPORT Pending Verification

Citizen Crowdsourced Signal

Reported by citizens in the area. Highlighted with confidence ratings and duplicate cluster indicators before deployment.

Example: "Unconfirmed report of tree across power lines on Oak St (4 reports)."
